About

From Mathematical Foundations to Intelligent Systems

I am a mathematician with a PhD in Mathematics and a research background in Graph Theory, currently expanding my academic and professional work toward Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.

My focus is on understanding and applying the mathematics behind modern computational methods — connecting areas such as linear algebra, mathematical modeling and analytical thinking with data-driven and intelligent systems.

Alongside research and academic leadership, I currently teach computationally focused courses including Linear Algebra for Machine Learning and Python for Mathematics, while developing further work in Machine Learning and AI.
