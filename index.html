01 / Perspective

My academic direction

Mathematics is not behind modern technology. It is inside it.

My work begins with mathematical structure: how objects relate, how networks can be distinguished and how complex systems can be represented.

I extend this perspective through research, teaching and academic development—helping students and collaborators connect rigorous mathematics with computation, data and intelligent systems.
