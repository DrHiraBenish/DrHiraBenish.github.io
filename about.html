Professional Profile

Mathematics as a Foundation for Data, Computing & AI

I am Dr. Hira Benish, a mathematician, researcher, educator and academic leader with a PhD in Mathematics specializing in Graph Theory. My current professional direction connects mathematical thinking with Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence and modern computational fields.

Professional Journey

From Mathematical Research to Modern Computational Applications

My academic foundation is rooted in Mathematics, Graph Theory and analytical problem solving. Over time, my teaching, research and professional development have increasingly moved toward computational mathematics, Python, Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.

I am particularly interested in understanding how mathematical structures and methods contribute to modern intelligent systems, and in helping students connect mathematical theory with practical computational applications.
