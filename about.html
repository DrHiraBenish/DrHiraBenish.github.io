A mathematician shaping the bridge between theory and intelligent systems.

Professional journey

Strong foundations. Evolving applications.

My academic identity is rooted in Mathematics, particularly Graph Theory, network analysis and analytical problem solving.

Over time, my research, teaching and curriculum development have increasingly engaged with Python, Data Science, Machine Learning and AI. This is not a departure from mathematics; it is an extension of its role in understanding, modelling and improving contemporary systems.

I aim to help students and collaborators see mathematics as an active language of technology, research, decision-making and social impact.
