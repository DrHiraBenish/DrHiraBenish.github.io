Biography

Mathematician · Educator · Academic Leader

Dr. Hira Benish

Dr. Hira Benish is an Associate Professor of Mathematics and Head of Department at Riphah International University, Sahiwal Campus. She holds a PhD in Mathematics with specialization in Graph Theory and has more than eight years of university teaching and academic leadership experience.

Her professional work connects mathematical foundations with Data Analytics, Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence through curriculum development, applied learning, research, faculty development and institutional initiatives. She also co-founded PMAI Lab to support public learning and collaboration across Mathematics, Python and AI.

Professional direction

Strong foundations. Evolving applications.

My academic identity is rooted in Graph Theory, network analysis and analytical problem-solving.

I now extend this foundation into Python, Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. This is not a departure from mathematics; it is an expansion of its role in understanding, modelling and improving contemporary systems.

Across teaching, research and leadership, my aim is to make mathematics more connected with technology, decision-making, careers and social impact.
