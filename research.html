Current research direction

Metric-coordinate features for graph learning.

I am investigating whether coordinates derived from a resolving set can provide neural models with explicit structural information that standard local aggregation may overlook.

The aim is to connect a classical Graph Theory concept with modern representation learning in a mathematically interpretable pipeline.
